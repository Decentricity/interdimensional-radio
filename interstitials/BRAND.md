# Interdimensional Radio

**Mascot:** Miss Hedgey Hog — a little pink hedgehog. She is the station mascot (brand character), **not** the DJ or announcer.

**Voices:**
- **Music3 ads & station IDs:** female (or male for legacy `station-id/voice/`) radio announcer — human broadcast voice, not the mascot.
- **Piper DJ chatter:** between-songs DJ patter — also not the mascot.

Miss Hedgey Hog may be **mentioned** in scripts (posters, booth decor, running gags) but never speaks the lines unless explicitly writing in-universe mascot sfx (we don't).

Keep scripts varied — avoid repeating neon/sci-fi imagery every line.
